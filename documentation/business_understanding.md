# Business Understanding

## Project Title

AdventureWorks Sales Analysis


## 1. Business Context

The objective of this project is to analyze sales performance using the
AdventureWorks2022 relational database and translate transactional data
into actionable business insights.

The analysis focuses on sales performance across products, customers,
sales territories, discounts, and time.


## 2. Business Problem

A business needs to understand what drives its sales performance and
where opportunities for improvement exist.

The available transactional data contains information about sales orders,
order details, customers, products, and sales territories. However, raw
transactional data alone does not provide clear answers to questions such
as which products generate the most revenue, which territories perform
best, or how discounts and customer concentration affect sales.

This project aims to transform the available data into meaningful
business insights that can support sales and commercial decision-making.


## 3. Project Objective

The main objective is to analyze sales performance and identify patterns,
high-performing areas, potential risks, and opportunities for improvement.

The analysis will use SQL Server and T-SQL for data exploration,
validation, transformation, and analysis, followed by visualization and
business interpretation.


## 4. Business Questions

### Q1 — Product Performance

Which products generate the highest revenue and sales volume?

### Q2 — Territory Performance

Which sales territories generate the highest revenue, and how does
performance vary across territories?

### Q3 — Customer Performance

Which customers contribute the most revenue, and how concentrated is
revenue among the top customers?

### Q4 — Discount Effectiveness

How do discounts relate to sales volume and revenue?

### Q5 — Sales Trends

How have sales revenue and order volume changed over time?


## 5. Scope

### In Scope

- Sales orders
- Sales order details
- Products
- Customers
- Sales territories
- Revenue
- Sales volume
- Discounts
- Order dates
- Product performance
- Customer performance
- Territory performance
- Sales trends

### Out of Scope

- Predictive sales forecasting
- Customer churn prediction
- Advanced machine learning
- Real-time sales monitoring
- External market data
- Detailed profitability analysis beyond the available data


## 6. Expected Business Outcomes

The analysis is expected to identify:

- High-performing products
- High-performing sales territories
- High-value customers
- Sales concentration patterns
- Discount-related sales patterns
- Important sales trends
- Potential areas for commercial improvement

The final recommendations will be based on the findings from the
analysis rather than assumptions made before examining the data.
